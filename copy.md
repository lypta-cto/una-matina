## How We Work
**H1:** Simple for you
**Lead:** There's a lot of production behind an event. Your side of it looks like this.
1. Inquiry — You tell us the date, the place, the guest count and what you have in mind.
2. Discovery — A call or a coffee about the people, the feel and the budget.
3. Concept & scope — We sketch a concept and agree what we handle.
4. Custom proposal — A written proposal built on your event's real scope. No packages.
5. Contract — We confirm the details and hold your date.
6. Planning & design — Vendors, layout, florals, menus and timeline come together.
7. Final production — Final confirmations, rentals arrive, flowers are arranged.
8. Event day — We set up, run the day and pack out.
9. Follow-up — We check in after the party.
Note: For beaches, parks and other public places we confirm the rules and permits before we finalise.
CTA: Start with step one
