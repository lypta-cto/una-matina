## Platform

web

## Stack
Nuxt 4 + Tailwind 4 (workspace starter). One project, two surfaces: the Events & Catering site and a standalone `/crepes` landing with its own layout. Static content in data files (inferred).

## Users
1. **Market visitor (surface B, `/crepes`).** Standing at or near the Una Mattina crepe stand at a Naples/SWFL farmers market, phone in hand, in bright sun, having scanned a QR code. Wants the menu and prices now, and to know when and where the stand will be next. Attention span: seconds.
2. **Private-event host (surface A).** Hosting a birthday, bridal or baby shower, engagement, intimate wedding, brunch or corporate gathering in Naples/SWFL, usually at a private home, backyard, beach or park. Wants a beautiful, fully handled event without coordinating several vendors. Often arrives from Instagram on mobile (inferred).

## Product Purpose
- Surface B: turn a QR scan into a visit to the stand and an Instagram follow.
- Surface A: turn interest into a completed inquiry (event type, date, location and location type, guest count, services of interest, message). Success = inquiries with a date and guest count (inferred).

## Positioning
One small team covers the whole event — planning and coordination, design with in-house florals, and live food stations prepared in front of guests — with no fixed packages; every event gets its own concept. The brand was born at the farmers market, so many clients will meet Una Mattina first as a crepe they ate on a Saturday morning.

## Operating Context
- Farmers markets in Naples/SWFL: a cream tent, chalkboard menu, striped apron and napkins, QR code on the stand.
- Event sites: private homes, backyards, poolside, beaches, parks, sunset locations. Public locations require confirming rules and permits before booking.
- Client journey (internal doc): Inquiry → Discovery → Concept + Scope → Custom Proposal → Contract → Planning + Design → Final Production → Event Day → Follow-up.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Three pillars, booked individually or combined: (1) Event Planning & Coordination, (2) Event Design, Florals & Setup, (3) Live Station Catering.
- Live stations: Crepe, Tiramisu Bar, Fruit Cup Bar, Charcuterie, Cannoli Bar, Cocktail/Mocktail Bar. Future: coffee/espresso, gelato, waffle/brunch, hot chocolate, lemonade.
- Special services: Luxury Picnics (beach, park, backyard, poolside, private property, sunset) and Bloom Cart (guests pick stems, build a small bouquet to take home).
- No public event prices; everything goes through inquiry. The crepe menu may show prices.
- Crepe menu is a proposal pending confirmation.
- Unknown and must be marked, never invented: market locations and schedule, phone, email, Instagram handle, domain, inquiry delivery backend, founder names/story.

## Brand Commitments
- Name and master logo shared across Crepes, Events & Catering and Café.
- Palette fixed: Cream #FFF7EA, Apricot #F4A878, Yellow #F6D67A, Sage #9EAD83, Brown #5B3A2E.
- Type fixed: Montserrat (Light, Regular) + Playfair Display Italic for accents; a script only for short sign-offs ("Good Food, Brighter Days"), never for body text.
- Motifs: the "A" mark with a rising sun (morning); the same mark upside down reads as a crepe cone (crepe); apricot and yellow cabana stripes from aprons and napkins.
- Taglines: "A Taste of Mediterranean Mornings", "Good Food, Brighter Days".
- Tone: warm, sunny, Mediterranean, elegant but relaxed. Not black-and-gold luxury wedding planner, not boho-rustic, not template event agency.

## Product Principles
1. One team, one concept: every page should make it obvious the client hands over the whole event, not a list of vendors.
2. Made in front of you: the live, hands-on moment (crepe on the griddle, stems chosen at the cart) is the brand's proof, more than décor.
3. Inquiry, not checkout: no packages, no prices for events; the form asks the few things needed to start a conversation.
4. Honest about being new: placeholders are labelled, nothing is invented.
5. The market stand page serves someone standing in the sun: fast, legible, one thumb.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast (palette's light tones never carry text on cream), legible outdoors in direct sun for `/crepes`, keyboard-usable inquiry form with labelled fields, reduced motion honoured.
